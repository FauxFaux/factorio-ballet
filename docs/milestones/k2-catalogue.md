[README.md](README.md) for accounting rules, route choices, commands, and interpretation.

Order follows the existing complexity model through electric furnaces, with flamethrower added
explicitly. Its military weighting is distorted by gathered biomass; percentages are reference
scores, not research requirements. Card vectors below are **basic / automation / logistic / military
/ chemical**.

## Technology choices and dead ends

routes. **Side** means optional for those two goals. **Leaf** means no technology in the entire
exported tree names it as a prerequisite. A leaf can still be useful (inserter settings, combat
bonuses, equipment, a better machine); complexity sorting does not make it compulsory. Closure costs
here count declared technology prerequisites only; blueprint scenarios additionally include material
and crafting-machine unlocks.
